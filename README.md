## About

I am a systems developer focused on transforming complex, manual business processes into reliable digital solutions.

My work combines **software development**, **process automation**, **system integration**, and **infrastructure**. I design and maintain internal platforms, workflow systems, operational dashboards, REST APIs, document-processing pipelines, and automation tools used in real business environments.

My main stack includes **PHP**, **Python**, **JavaScript**, and **SQL**. I also work with Linux servers, Nginx, GitHub Actions, cloud services, and production deployment workflows.

Currently, I am building and standardizing the **Nexus Platform** ecosystem: a group of business applications designed to centralize operations, improve visibility, and reduce repetitive work.

## Selected Projects

### Nexus Platform

A growing ecosystem of business applications designed to centralize internal tools, standardize user experience, and support operational workflows under a shared architecture.

### Nexus Pulse

A ticket and service-management platform featuring Kanban workflows, integrated chat, role-based access control, notifications, workspaces, administration modules, and a scalable product-oriented structure.

### SSW Automation Suite

A Python-based automation ecosystem for transportation operations, including browser workflows, report generation, document processing, operational metrics, data consolidation, and internal web interfaces.

### Purchasing Management System

A corporate purchasing platform with requests, quotations, hierarchical approvals, supplier management, cost allocation, auditing, follow-up stages, and Kanban-based tracking.

### PRO.ON

An internal company portal that centralizes administrative resources, operational tools, business applications, and department-specific modules in a unified environment.

> Some of these systems support private company operations. Public case studies and technical showcases are being prepared without exposing proprietary code or business data.
